The Empire Wand is back: the Bukkit plugin from 2013, rebuilt for NeoForge (Minecraft 26.3 and 26.1.2).

- **Thirty spells.** The fifteen from 2013 (Explode, Lightning Arrow, Escape, Launch, Leap, Confuse, Capture, Little Spark, Comet, Spark, Jump, Poison Wave, Explosive, Blood Block, Lightning Storm) and fifteen new ones, from Meteor Shower to Black Hole.
- **Right-click** picks a spell, **left-click** casts it; hold left-click to charge a bigger cast. After each spell the wand cools down: a second for a spark, up to fifteen for a black hole.
- **The spell wheel** (R), favourites on 1 to 5, sneak and scroll.
- **Spell scrolls** in chests, vaults, suspicious blocks and on mobs, each place with its kind of spell; sealed scrolls from librarians.
- **Combos:** Shatter, Storm, Toxic Tornado.
- **The spellbook, enchantments** (Power, Reach, Multicast) and **skins** (gold, obsidian, bone).
- **The Dark Mage and the Wizard Tower.**
- **Advancements.**
